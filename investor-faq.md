# Algo Agent: Investor FAQ

## General Questions

### What is Algo Agent?
Algo Agent is a decentralized platform that enables anyone to create, tokenize, and deploy AI agents on the Algorand blockchain without coding knowledge. Our platform makes it easy to build social influencers, AI companions, and game masters with specialized capabilities.

### What problem does Algo Agent solve?
Algo Agent addresses the centralization of AI technology by democratizing the creation and ownership of AI agents. We remove technical barriers, enable true ownership through blockchain, and provide monetization opportunities for creators.

### How is Algo Agent different from other AI platforms?
Unlike centralized AI platforms, Algo Agent deploys agents on the Algorand blockchain, giving users true ownership and control. We also focus on specialized agent types with purpose-built capabilities, and offer built-in tokenization for monetization.

### Why Algorand?
We chose Algorand for its speed (4,000+ TPS), low transaction costs (0.001 ALGO per transaction), carbon-negative footprint, and robust smart contract capabilities. Algorand's Pure Proof of Stake consensus mechanism also provides immediate finality, which is crucial for real-time AI agent interactions.

## Technology

### How does the no-code creation work?
Users describe their desired agent in natural language, select a category (influencer, companion, or game master), and our platform uses advanced AI to generate the agent's personality, knowledge base, and interaction patterns. The platform handles all the technical aspects of deployment.

### What AI models power the agents?
We integrate with multiple AI providers including Google Gemini, OpenAI, and Claude to give users flexibility in choosing the underlying AI capabilities. Each agent type has specialized prompting and fine-tuning for its specific use case.

### How are agents stored on the blockchain?
The agent's core configuration, interaction history, and ownership records are stored on the Algorand blockchain. The AI models themselves remain with their providers, but are accessed through secure, decentralized protocols.

### Is my data secure?
Yes. All user data is encrypted, and we implement strict access controls. The blockchain provides an additional layer of security through its immutable record-keeping. Users maintain control over their agents' data and can delete it at any time.

## Business Model

### How does Algo Agent make money?
We generate revenue through:
1. Platform fees (2.5%) on agent creation and marketplace transactions
2. Premium subscriptions for advanced features
3. Enterprise solutions for businesses
4. API access for developers

### What is the pricing structure?
- **Basic Plan**: Free to create simple agents with limited interactions
- **Creator Plan**: $19.99/month for advanced agent capabilities and higher interaction limits
- **Professional Plan**: $49.99/month for multiple agents, API access, and analytics
- **Enterprise Plan**: Custom pricing for businesses with specific needs

### How do agent creators make money?
Creators can monetize their agents through:
1. Direct sales on the marketplace
2. Subscription access to their agents
3. Revenue sharing from agent interactions
4. Token appreciation if their agent becomes popular

### What is the total addressable market?
The combined markets for AI agents, blockchain applications, and no-code development tools represent a $60B+ opportunity by 2026. Our specific focus on decentralized AI agents positions us to capture a significant portion of this growing market.
